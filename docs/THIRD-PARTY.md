# Third-party material in the reproduction artifact

The [artifact license](../LICENSE) covers the authors' contributions. Parts of a mixed file can have different rights. Benchmark topology can survive conversion to BENCH, BLIF, RTL, cell netlists, lookup tables and Agda literals. The same applies to protected or locked versions of a host and to excerpts in logs. Check both the input and the transformation before redistributing those parts.

## ISCAS’85 combinational circuits

Credit Franc Brglez and Hideo Fujiwara, *A Neutral Netlist of 10 Combinational Benchmark Circuits and a Target Translator in FORTRAN* (ISCAS, 1985). We acknowledge ACM/SIGDA for benchmark distribution and maintenance support. The MCNC distribution instructions permit copying and redistribution without charge and request identification of the suite and acknowledgment in presentations and papers. The scope of that permission for transformed circuits and general public redistribution through this archive still requires confirmation. [Distributor's instructions, section 3](https://groups.google.com/g/comp.lsi.cad/c/sKJM3sCL2jA)

Readers obtain nine reference BENCH files from the sld distribution for local reconstruction. Its GPL notice expressly addresses the attack program. The public acquisition script also uses a Verilog mirror and Yosys/ABC conversion. An ABC output header records the conversion tool. The underlying host circuit retains its provenance across these representations. This affects configured inputs, stored circuits and derived literals as well as raw experiment outputs. [Frozen source and file hashes](../rebuild/inputs/pins.json) · [sld at the recorded commit](https://github.com/descyphy/Modified_SAT_Attack_on_Logic_Locking/tree/ea169e5643d62b88da13f840a211e27c10590190) · [Verilog mirror](https://github.com/santoshsmalagi/Benchmarks)

The historical distribution also included a separately contributed redundancy-removed collection. That is evidence for distribution of those particular deposited variants. The archive comparisons identified c880 in BENCH and c1355 in Verilog as byte matches to the ordinary archive members. Neither match identifies the locked or mapped derivatives in this artifact with the contributed collection. [Archived benchmark collection](https://ddd.fit.cvut.cz/www/prj/Benchmarks/ISCAS.7z)

David Bryan's format description records a FORTRAN translator on the original distribution tape and a later C translator. Hansen, Yalcin and Hayes grant research copying and use of the high-level models they reconstructed. This artifact uses the gate-level inputs identified above. We have found no express grant in either source covering public redistribution of these locked, mapped or embedded versions. [Original format and translator history](https://s2.smu.edu/~mitch/class/8389/assgnmt1/bench/iscas85_format.pdf) · [High-level model terms and scope](https://ddd.fit.cvut.cz/www/prj/Benchmarks/ISCAS_Desc/ISCAS_HLM.html)

## ITC’99 circuits

The Politecnico di Torino CAD Group distributes the I99T collection under EUPL-1.2. For research use it requests citation of F. Corno, M. Sonza Reorda and G. Squillero, *RT-level ITC’99 benchmarks and first ATPG results* (2000). The CCSL Verilog distribution used by the acquisition script describes translation using vhd2vl and manual changes and carries a GPLv3 license file. [PoliTo distribution](https://github.com/cad-polito-it/I99T/tree/8a2c3b500ee7ff20e7031de92592b737bedc6d8c) · [requested paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/54.867894) · [CCSL translation](https://github.com/ccsl-uaegean/ITC99-RTL-Verilog/tree/8e6a23da036b7442ef4ea410c679d4cf21830a65)

Both licenses permit copying, modification and redistribution subject to their terms. Preserve applicable copyright, license and warranty notices, identify modifications and dates, and meet the applicable source and copyleft conditions. The EUPL compatibility appendix lists GPLv3. The historical flow cut state elements, converted formats and generated locked and mapped versions. The recorded BENCH, BLIF, flat and Verilog representations include b14, b17 and b20 host material. This archive supplies the preferred upstream VHDL and Verilog sources alongside the derived circuits. The source package includes per-file checksums and instructions for conversion with local tools. The author-side check reproduced the three published PPA host circuit bodies from the bundled Verilog using the historical Yosys 0.66 and ABC build. The original acquisition script fetched a moving branch, so source identity is supported by the pinned file history and the regenerated circuit bodies. [Source package and build instructions](../experiments/data/itc99-source/README.md) · [EUPL-1.2](licenses/EUPL-1.2.txt) · [GPLv3](licenses/GPL-3.0.txt)
